# Suggested Additional Pages for Academic Center Management System

Based on the "Parent-First" concept and common academic system functionalities, here are some additional pages that could be included:

*   `student_profile.jsp`: Detailed view for parents to see their child's information.
*   `view_grades.jsp`: Allows parents to view their children's grades.
*   `view_attendance.jsp`: Provides a historical record of a child's attendance.
*   `messages.jsp`: Internal messaging system for parents to communicate with teachers or administration.
*   `announcements.jsp`: Displays school-wide or class-specific announcements.
*   `class_schedule.jsp`: Shows the daily or weekly schedule for a student or a specific class.
*   `teacher_list.jsp`: (For admin/staff) To manage and view teacher information.
*   `course_management.jsp`: (For admin/staff) To add, edit, or view courses offered.
